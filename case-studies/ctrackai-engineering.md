# CTrackAI Product & Engineering Overview 🍎

## Shipped Product

CTrackAI is a consumer calorie and nutrition tracker I built and shipped for iOS and Android.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/ctrackai-free-calorie-tracker/id6758379785) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ctrackaiai.nutrition)

The public listings describe photo-based food logging, barcode scanning, calorie and macro tracking, meal planning, fasting and hydration tracking. The App Store also publishes a version history and supported languages. These are public release references and developer-provided feature descriptions, rather than an independent evaluation of nutrition accuracy.

## Engineering Scope

The product brings together mobile input, AI-assisted food interpretation and daily nutrition records. Its portfolio value is the shipped consumer experience: getting from food input to a usable log, then making that history understandable over time.

The commercial source is not published with this overview. This page therefore does not specify an internal caching algorithm, model version, storage implementation or purchase-validation design as a verified fact about the distributed app.

## Measurement Boundaries

A defensible performance or cost result needs a dated report tied to a build, device, sample, workload and comparison baseline. No such report is published here, so this overview does not quote capture latency, cache hit latency, API savings, session counts or crash-free percentages.

Ratings vary by storefront and time; the linked store pages are the source for current ratings. AI-derived nutrition values should be treated as estimates, and this overview makes no clinical-accuracy claim.

## Privacy & Release Evidence

The [App Store privacy section](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/ctrackai-free-calorie-tracker/id6758379785) and [Google Play data-safety section](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ctrackaiai.nutrition) publish developer-provided disclosures.
