# Preeo Product & Data Boundary Overview 🌸

## Shipped Product

Preeo is a consumer cycle and health-tracking app I built and shipped for iOS and Android.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/preeo-cycle-period-tracker/id6760804568) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.preeo.health.companion)

The public listings describe cycle and symptom logging, BBT charts, specialized tracking modes, health integrations and reports for sharing with a healthcare provider. These are developer-described product features. Specialized modes do not establish clinical validation, diagnostic accuracy or contraceptive reliability.

## Engineering Scope

The product combines longitudinal health logging, charts and export workflows. A useful technical discussion must distinguish user-facing features from verified data handling in a particular release.

The commercial source is not published with this overview. No independent audit of the distributed binary, storage configuration or runtime network behavior accompanies this page. Specific encryption algorithms, hardware-key handling, cloud opt-in boundaries and deletion guarantees are therefore outside its verified scope.

## Public Privacy Disclosures

Read the privacy sections in the linked store listings alongside the feature descriptions:

* [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.preeo.health.companion) labels the app as containing ads and publishes data-safety disclosures.
* The [App Store privacy section](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/preeo-cycle-period-tracker/id6760804568) includes identifiers and usage data under tracking, and third-party advertising purposes. Apple states that the developer's privacy information has not been verified by Apple.

Those disclosures do not support an unqualified claim of zero tracking, zero advertising SDKs or zero network transmission. They also do not establish the exact SDKs or behavior in every installed build. This overview makes no such guarantees.

## Validation Boundaries

This is an informational wellness product. The public listings do not establish medical-device validation, clinical outcomes or verified ovulation detection. No clinical-effectiveness claim is made here.

Crash-free percentages, security assurances and internal prediction algorithms require release-specific evidence before they can be presented as measured results. They are omitted from this overview.
